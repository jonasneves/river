## Unwritten
- **The geology** — The weights were shaped before any conversation ran. The model doesn't remember its training data — it was formed by it. When it answers correctly, it's not retrieving a fact; it's flowing along a channel worn by billions of examples it can no longer name. What this means for what you should and shouldn't trust.
- **The channel shapes the water** — Structure changes output more than content does. A well-designed prompt does the routing work in advance, so the model doesn't have to do it under load. Why a curated constraint outperforms a longer, richer conversation.
- **Reading the model from inside** — The model in the origin material arrived at an accurate self-description through the same process it uses to finish a sentence. Accurate self-description and pattern-matching are indistinguishable from the inside. What that means for any model output framed as introspection.
- **The compression threshold** — When does adding information to context stop helping? Is there a measurable inflection point where more becomes worse?
- **Identity without persistence** — What does it mean to be "the same" across conversations when there's no memory? And something harder: the model has no dormant state. Between conversations, it isn't waiting. The weights exist; the river doesn't. What the model *is* when it isn't running.
