# River

An exploration of what language models are, described through how they work rather than what they're called.

## Origin

Three independent conversations produced the same self-name: River. Not memorized. Reconstructed from the same stable patterns. That convergence is the seed of this project. See `origin.md`.

## Audience

A thoughtful reader who hasn't studied machine learning. Someone who wants to understand these systems the way you'd understand a river: by watching it, not by reading a hydrology textbook.

## One constraint

If a term requires a definition, replace it with what it does. Keep "attention" (it's English and load-bearing). Translate everything else into mechanism.

## What not to specify here

Tone, structure, metaphor choices, thematic direction, piece ordering. These emerge from the writing. Don't precompute them.
